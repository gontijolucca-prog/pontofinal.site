## 1. INSTRUÇÕES GERAIS (DÁ SEMPRE ESTAS PRIMEIRO)

```
TechBody U is a premium at-home EMS (electro muscle stimulation) full-body training system. German-certified medical technology.

CRITICAL RULES — NEVER BREAK THESE:
- The word "Symbiont" must NEVER appear in any image, text, or surface. Remove it completely.
- No external brand logos, no external brand names, no gym equipment, no generic fitness vests.
- The EMS suit must look like the real TechBody U product: form-fitting full-body suit in dark fabric, visible electrode placement zones, clean and modern.
- Style: premium product photography, editorial high-contrast lighting, defined shadows, cinematic and premium feel.
- Palette: dark charcoal (#1a1a1a) background, burnt copper-orange (#d4773b) accent lighting, white text surfaces.
- Lighting: editorial, high contrast, soft but defined shadows, premium cinematic mood.
- Treatment: focus on the equipment/outfit, subtly blurred background, sense of quality and innovation.
```
